Dolphins are known for their intelligence and problem-solving skills. 2. 😴 Sleep with one eye open! Dolphins can rest one half of their brain while the other half stays alert. 3. 🗣️ They have names! Dolphins use unique whistles to identify themselves, almost like names. 4. 🐟 Fish lovers! Many dolphins eat fish and squid. 5. 🎶 Chatty creatures! Dolphins often live and travel in groups called pods. 8. 🫧 Bubble fun! Dolphins sometimes blow bubble rings underwater. 9. 🪞 Recognize themselves! 1. 🌊 No brain! Jellyfish have no brain, heart, or bones. 2. ✨ Ancient animals! Jellyfish have existed for at least 500 million years. 3. 💧 Mostly water! Their bodies are made up of roughly 95% water, depending on the species. 4. 🦵 No legs! They drift and swim using their soft, jelly-like bodies. 5. ⚡ Stinging superpowers! Many jellyfish use stinging cells to catch prey. 6. 🌈 Rainbow beauties! Some jellyfish glow or look colorful under certain lighting. 7. 🍽️ Tiny hunters! They eat animals such as plankton, tiny crustaceans, and small fish. 8. 🌍 Ocean travelers! Many jellyfish drift with ocean currents. 9. 👀 Some can see! Certain jellyfish have eyes or light-sensing organs. 10. ♻️ Life-cycle surprise! Some jellyfish species can return to an earlier life stage under certain conditions. 🐋 10 Fun Facts About Belugas

1. 🤍 White whales! Belugas are famous for their beautiful white skin as adults. 2. 🐣 Born gray! Baby belugas are usually gray or dark gray, not white. 3. 😊 Flexible faces! Their necks are more flexible than those of most other whales, helping them turn their heads. 4. 🗣️ Sea canaries! Belugas make lots of whistles, clicks, squeaks, and chirps. 5. 🎈 Big forehead! Their rounded forehead is called a melon and helps them focus sounds. 6. ❄️ Arctic experts! Belugas live mainly in Arctic and sub-Arctic waters. 7. 🐟 Fishy meals! They eat fish, squid, shrimp, and other sea animals. 8. 👨‍👩‍👧 Social swimmers! Belugas often gather in groups. 9. 🌬️ Need air! Like dolphins, belugas are mammals and must surface to breathe. 10. 🦷 No horn! Unlike narwhals, belugas do not have a long tusk.
