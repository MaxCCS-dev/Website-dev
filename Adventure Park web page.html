Did you know

🦕 Amazing Dinosaur Facts 1. 🦖 T. rex had tiny arms! Despite being a giant predator, the Tyrannosaurus rex had surprisingly short arms with two fingers on each hand. 2. 🥚 Dinosaurs laid eggs! Many dinosaurs built nests and laid eggs, just like modern birds. 3. 🐦 Birds are living dinosaurs! Modern birds evolved from a group of feathered dinosaurs called theropods. 4. 🦕 Some dinosaurs were HUGE! Long-necked dinosaurs, such as Argentinosaurus, may have reached around 30–35 meters in length. 5. 🦴 Triceratops had three horns! It had two large horns above its eyes and one smaller horn on its nose. 6. 🪶 Some dinosaurs had feathers! Scientists have discovered fossils showing that many dinosaurs had feathers or feather-like coverings. 7. 🌍 Dinosaurs lived millions of years ago! Non-avian dinosaurs lived during the Mesozoic Era, which lasted from about 252 to 66 million years ago. 8. 🛡️ Stegosaurus had plates on its back! Its back was covered with large bony plates, and it had four spikes on its tail. 9. 🏃 Some dinosaurs were very fast! Small, lightweight dinosaurs could run quickly to catch prey or escape predators. 10. ☄️ An asteroid helped end the age of dinosaurs! About 66 million years ago, a giant asteroid struck Earth, contributing to the extinction of non-avian dinosaurs.🦕 Did you know? The word dinosaur means “terrible lizard,” even though dinosaurs were not actually lizards

🧠 Some dinosaurs had tiny brains! Stegosaurus had a relatively small brain compared with its enormous body.
