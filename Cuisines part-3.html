Fun facts about noodles

🥢 Noodles are thousands of years old! Some of the earliest known noodles were discovered in China. 🍜 Ramen originated in Japan and has become famous all around the world. ⚡ Instant noodles were invented in 1958 by Momofuku Ando in Japan. 🎂 Long noodles can symbolize long life, so they are sometimes eaten at birthdays and celebrations. 🍜 There are hundreds of noodle varieties with different shapes, sizes, colors, and textures. 🥔 Noodles aren't always made from wheat—they can also be made from rice, buckwheat, potato starch, and sweet potato starch. 🇰🇷 Korean glass noodles are often made from sweet potato starch and become wonderfully chewy when cooked. 🌀 Hand-pulled noodles are made by repeatedly stretching dough into long strands. 🌶️ Noodles can be sweet, spicy, sour, salty, or savory, depending on the recipe. 🌎 Noodles are loved worldwide! From Japanese ramen and Vietnamese pho to Italian spaghetti, almost every region has its own noodle dishes. ❤️🍜
