Famous monuments

Famous monuments around the world hide surprising stories, secret rooms, and strange engineering choices behind their grand appearances.

FUN FACTS ABOUT Famous monuments around the world!!

Eiffel Tower(Paris, France) Secret apartment: Creator Gustave Eiffel built a small private apartment for himself at the very top.Temporary design: The tower was meant to be torn down after 20 years, but saved because it worked well as a radio antenna.Growing height: Metal on the tower expands in the summer heat, making the structure grow by up to 15 centimeters.

Sticky rice mortar: Workers used a mixture of cooked sticky rice and slaked lime to bind the bricks together, making the mortar exceptionally strong.Not one continuous line: The landmark is actually a vast collection of separate walls, trenches, and natural defensive hills built over many dynasties.

Colosseum (Rome, Italy) Underground zoo: Beneath the wooden floor of the main arena lies a complex maze of tunnels called the hypogeum.Animal elevators: Gladiators and wild beasts waited down below and were hoisted up into the fight using a system of ropes and crude hand-powered elevators.

Statue of Liberty (New York, USA) Copper skin: The outer green layer is super thin—only as thick as two pennies stacked together (about 2.4 millimeters).Lightning rod: The massive copper statue acts as a giant lightning conductor, getting struck roughly 600 times every year.

Big Ben (London, UK) "Big Ben" is actually the name of the massive 13.7-ton bell inside, not the actual clock tower itself (which was renamed the Elizabeth Tower in 2012).

Tower of Pisa (Italy) Its famous lean actually saved it from being destroyed during World War II because the occupying troops used it as a vantage point.

The Great Pyramid of Giza (Egypt) It is not completely flat on top; it has a small plateau, and it is the only wonder of the ancient world that is still mostly intact.

Taj Mahal (India) The main mausoleum is completely symmetrical, except for the tombs inside—Shah Jahan's tomb is placed off-center to the side, as it was added later next to his wife's.
